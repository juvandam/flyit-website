    About This Aircraft

    This Cessna 310R is an excellent opportunity to acquire a perfectly maintained twin-engine aircraft, equipped with modern Garmin avionics and exceptional performance.

    Built in 1976, this twin combines the proven reliability of a Cessna airframe with the capability of a twin-engine aircraft. Powered by two Continental IO-520-MB engines at 285 horsepower each, it offers exceptional climb performance, 55% fuel efficiency, excellent cruise performance and strong high-altitude capability.

    Equipped with a complete modern Garmin avionics suite, including two primary displays, an audio panel supporting external connections, GTN GPS units and autopilot. A Starlink antenna provides in-flight weather updates and internet access.

    The aircraft underwent a full overhaul in June 2026, including landing gear, engines and propellers. Ready to fly. It can be delivered anywhere in the world by a ferry-flight pilot.
