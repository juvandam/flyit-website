    Frequently Asked Questions

    What types of aircraft do you bring into Argentina?

    Single-engine, twin-engine and turboprop aircraft, mainly general aviation, operational and light commercial types.

    How long does the full process take?

    8-12 months on average, from listing to delivery. It depends on how quickly we match a buyer, negotiation speed, and ANAC's current workload. Every aircraft is different.

    Do you charge a commission to list an aircraft?

    We'll walk you through our terms directly once you get in touch — costs and fees are always disclosed up front, with no hidden surprises.

    What documentation do I need to provide as a seller?

    Aircraft logbooks, maintenance records, current registration and airworthiness documents. We guide you through exactly what's needed once you list your aircraft.
